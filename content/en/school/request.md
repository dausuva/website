We can demonstrate Classroom Manager **on site in Vilnius County**, or arrange a **Zoom call with questions and answers** anywhere. After the demo, we can prepare an individual package for your school.

Tell us your school or organization, country, preferred demo format, contact name, number of Windows PCs and labs, and the tasks you want to simplify. No account is required.

The buttons below open a draft in your email app; nothing is submitted automatically. You can also copy the address or call. Please do not include passwords, access keys or student records.

Classroom Manager is in early access. We will agree the package version, pilot scope and unresolved limits before you deploy.
