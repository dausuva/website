## What Classroom Manager does not do

The product does not view, capture or record student screens. It is a PC-management tool, not a screen-surveillance service.

## What it does handle

Depending on the action used, the Host and Clients handle computer and user names, connection status, hardware and software inventory, processes and window titles, local and signed-in application accounts, websites open in browsers, commands and their output, task results, configuration, logs and files you send or collect. User names, browser information and collected work may identify people.

Control traffic and collected files are handled on the school's computers, not through a Dausuva Labs cloud control service. This does **not** mean every action is offline: optional release checks, software sources, Windows Update and runtime downloads can contact external services.

## Privacy policy of the Classroom Manager apps

This is the privacy policy of the Classroom Manager **Host** (the teacher's program, including the Microsoft Store version) and the **Client** (the program on student computers). Last updated 4 November 2026.

- **Who publishes them:** Dausuva Labs, the name under which Dausuva MB (registration code 307580523, Vytauto g. 22-22A, LT-90123 Plungė, Lithuania) publishes the software. Contact: {{< contact >}}.
- **What the apps send to us:** nothing. They have no account, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry, and they do not contact any server of ours.
- **What leaves the computers:** the Host talks only to the computers the teacher added, over the school network, encrypted and authenticated with a key that stays on your computers. Once a day the Host may look at github.com to see whether a newer version exists; it asks the first time it starts and you can switch it off. That request is an ordinary web request, so GitHub sees the computer's internet address. Windows, the Microsoft Store and any software source you choose have their own policies.
- **What is stored, and where:** on the teacher's computer, the Host's settings, the list of computers, keys, logs and any files you collect; on a student computer, the Client's configuration, keys and logs. A school's licence file is stored on the teacher's computer and is not sent anywhere. The Microsoft Store version keeps its settings inside the app's own storage, which Windows removes when you uninstall it; the other versions follow the uninstaller's choice.
- **What the apps can see on a managed computer:** the data listed above, such as names, inventory, processes, window titles, accounts and files you choose to collect. They do not view, capture or record screens.
- **Children:** the apps are not made for children to use. They are used by schools and administrators, who decide what is collected about students, why, how long it is kept, and who is told; the school is responsible for that data.
- **Your rights:** for data we hold about you (for example when you contact us), write to {{< contact >}}. You may ask to see, correct or delete it, and you may complain to the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (vdai.lrv.lt).
- **Changes:** this page shows the date of the last change.

## Your school makes the policy

Decide who may administer PCs, which files may be collected, where results and logs are stored, how long they are retained, and how staff, students and parents are informed. The software does not set a universal retention period or establish your school's lawful basis.

An Admin can run privileged commands and deploy software. Technical permissions and staff policy must work together.

## Support and demonstrations

Send only the minimum information needed for support. Remove personal data from screenshots and logs; never email access keys or student files without a separately agreed, appropriate process.

Website visits and demo enquiries are separate from product operation. Read the [website privacy notice]({{< relref "/privacy" >}}) for contact and hosting information.
