## What Classroom Manager does not do

The product does not view, capture or record student screens. It is a PC-management tool, not a screen-surveillance service.

## What it does handle

Depending on the action used, the Host and Clients handle computer and user names, connection status, hardware and software inventory, processes and window titles, local and signed-in application accounts, websites open in browsers, commands and their output, task results, configuration, logs and files you send or collect. User names, browser information and collected work may identify people.

Control traffic and collected files are handled on the school's computers, not through a Dausuva Labs cloud control service. This does **not** mean every action is offline: optional release checks, software sources, Windows Update and runtime downloads can contact external services.

## Privacy policy of the Classroom Manager apps

This policy covers the Classroom Manager **Host** (the teacher's program, including the Microsoft Store version) and the **Client** (the program on student computers). It does not cover this website; see the [website privacy notice]({{< relref "/privacy" >}}). Last updated 4 November 2026.

### The short version

- The apps send **nothing** to us: no account, no advertising, no analytics, no telemetry.
- The apps do not view, capture or record screens.
- The data the apps handle stays on the school's own computers. The school decides what is collected, why, and for how long.

### Who is responsible for the data

The school (or whoever runs the Host) decides why and how the data below is handled, so the school is responsible for it, including informing staff, students and parents and choosing a lawful basis. We do not receive that data and cannot see it. If a school sends us data to get support, we use it only to help that school and on its instructions, and we will sign a data-processing agreement on request.

### What the apps handle on the school's computers

Depending on the action a teacher uses: computer and user names, network and hardware details, installed programs, running processes and window titles, local and signed-in application accounts, websites open in browsers (the domain and window title), commands and their output, task results, settings, logs, and files that are sent or collected. Some of this can identify a person, and a window title or a file can contain anything a student was doing.

Where it is kept: the Host keeps its settings, computer list, keys, logs and collected files on the teacher's computer; the Client keeps its configuration, keys and a rolling log on each student computer. Much of it is only shown on screen and not stored. The apps set no automatic deadline for deleting exported files, collected files or logs: the school decides how long to keep them and deletes them. Uninstalling removes the programs; the Microsoft Store version also removes its settings, which live in the app's own storage. A school's licence file stays on the teacher's computer and is not sent anywhere.

### What we receive

Only what you choose to send us. Writing to us, or using "Email support now" in the Host, opens a message in your own email program; the Host does not send it for you, and it includes nothing you did not write. We use what you send only to answer you and to prepare what you asked for, and keep it no longer than that and our legal obligations require. Please do not send keys, passwords, or student names or files.

### Network connections

The Host and Client talk to each other over the school's network, encrypted (TLS) and authenticated with a key. Beyond that, the Host may once a day ask github.com whether a newer version exists (it asks the first time it starts, and you can switch it off); like any web request, this shows the computer's internet address to GitHub. Windows Update, the Microsoft Store, runtime downloads and any software source an administrator chooses are separate services with their own policies. We do not transfer personal data to other countries, because we do not receive it.

### Security

Connections use TLS and a shared key that stays on your computers; keys are protected for the Windows account that owns them. No software is perfectly secure. Report a security problem to {{< contact >}}; do not post it publicly first.

### Children

The apps are not made for children to use. They are used by schools and administrators to manage school computers. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

### Your rights

For data we hold about you (for example when you write to us) you may ask to see, correct or delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive a copy, by writing to {{< contact >}}. For data a school holds, contact the school. You may complain to the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (vdai.lrv.lt) or to the authority of your own country.

### Changes

If this policy changes, this page shows the new date. This policy describes what the software does and is not legal advice for your school.

## Your school makes the policy

Decide who may administer PCs, which files may be collected, where results and logs are stored, how long they are retained, and how staff, students and parents are informed. The software does not set a universal retention period or establish your school's lawful basis.

An Admin can run privileged commands and deploy software. Technical permissions and staff policy must work together.

## Support and demonstrations
