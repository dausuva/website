Several kinds of tools overlap with Classroom Manager, and schools often meet all of them: **classroom products** that show and steer student screens (Veyon, NetSupport School, Faronics Insight, LanSchool, Impero Education Pro), **Microsoft's own tools** that set PCs up and lock them down for a test, **remote management tools** that look after a fleet of computers (Tactical RMM), and **remote desktop tools** used for help desk work. Classroom Manager sits between them: the teacher's tools for a Windows lab, with the administrator's tools beside them, and deliberately no viewing or recording of student screens.

## The comparisons

- **[Veyon]({{< relref "/school/compare-veyon" >}})**: the best-known free classroom tool. Veyon is about seeing and steering screens; Classroom Manager is about preparing and maintaining the lab.
- **[NetSupport School]({{< relref "/school/compare-netsupport-school" >}})**: a commercial suite for teaching with screens, tests and sharing.
- **[Faronics Insight]({{< relref "/school/compare-faronics-insight" >}})**: a commercial suite with monitoring alerts, built-in tests and polls, for mixed devices.
- **[LanSchool]({{< relref "/school/compare-lanschool" >}})**: a classroom suite with a locally hosted edition and a cloud edition, and class-roster integrations.
- **[Impero Education Pro]({{< relref "/school/compare-impero-education-pro" >}})**: classroom management combined with safeguarding.
- **[Microsoft's school PC tools]({{< relref "/school/compare-windows-take-a-test" >}})**: set a PC up once and lock it down for a test; not a classroom program.
- **[Tactical RMM]({{< relref "/school/compare-tactical-rmm" >}})**: the free self-hosted management tool for an administrator with a server.
- **[Remote desktop tools]({{< relref "/school/compare-remote-tools" >}})**: TeamViewer, AnyDesk, RustDesk and DWService reach one computer; a classroom needs one action sent to all.
