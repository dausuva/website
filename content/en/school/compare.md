Three kinds of tools overlap with Classroom Manager, and schools often meet all of them: **classroom tools** that show and steer student screens (Veyon, NetSupport School) and **remote management tools** that look after a fleet of computers (Tactical RMM). Classroom Manager sits between them: the teacher's tools for a Windows lab, with the administrator's tools beside them, and deliberately no viewing or recording of student screens.

| | Kind of tool | Built around | Student screens | How it is priced |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [**Veyon**]({{< relref "/school/compare-veyon" >}}) | Classroom tool, open source | Watching and steering screens during a lesson | Thumbnails, remote view and control, screen broadcast | Free core; add-ons are priced on request |
| [**NetSupport School**]({{< relref "/school/compare-netsupport-school" >}}) | Commercial classroom suite | Teaching with screens: monitoring, sharing, tests; a Technician's Console for IT | Live views, with app, website and keyboard activity | A quote by number of devices; a "buy once" option; no list prices |
| [**Tactical RMM**]({{< relref "/school/compare-tactical-rmm" >}}) | Remote monitoring and management, self-hosted | An administrator watching and repairing a fleet | Remote desktop to one computer; no classroom view | Free for your own systems; commercial services need the vendor's approval |
| **Classroom Manager** | Teacher and lab tool for Windows | Preparing, running a lesson in, and maintaining a Windows lab | Deliberately none | Free for up to 3 computers online at once; for a school €499 the first year, then €299 a year |

## The comparisons

- **[Classroom Manager and Veyon]({{< relref "/school/compare-veyon" >}})**: the best-known free classroom tool. Veyon is about seeing and steering screens; Classroom Manager is about preparing and maintaining the lab.
- **[Classroom Manager and NetSupport School]({{< relref "/school/compare-netsupport-school" >}})**: the commercial classroom suite. NetSupport School is about teaching with screens, tests and sharing; Classroom Manager is about the Windows lab and a published flat price.
- **[Classroom Manager and Tactical RMM]({{< relref "/school/compare-tactical-rmm" >}})**: the free self-hosted management tool. Tactical RMM is for the administrator with a server and an alerting fleet; Classroom Manager is for the teacher with a lab.
