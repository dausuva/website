Follow the course in order or choose the task you need today. Each lesson has its own video, a written explanation and timestamps so you can return to a specific step.

The course is for teachers and school IT staff working with Windows 10 and Windows 11 computers. The videos use the English interface and English synthetic narration, with English and Lithuanian subtitle files prepared for every lesson. Administrative changes are identified as previews where they are not applied in the recording.
