**The argument.** When people use something wrongly, the design is at fault. Good design makes it plain what can be done, what is happening and what just happened, and it forgives mistakes.

**Ideas worth keeping.** Discoverability, signifiers, feedback, a correct mental model, constraints, and recovery from error.

**Where it shows in Classroom Manager.** Clear labels with icons, a help guide with real screenshots, confirmations before risky actions such as Exam mode, and changes that undo themselves when nobody confirms.
