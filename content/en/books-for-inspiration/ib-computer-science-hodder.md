**The argument.** This is a textbook for students taking IB Diploma Computer Science. It follows the revised course, which has its first assessment in 2027, and covers both Standard Level and Higher Level. The course sets out concepts in computer science alongside computational thinking, problem solving and programming, so the book is a good first map of the whole field.

## Ideas worth keeping

- **Concepts of computer science**, such as how computers, networks and data work.
- **Computational thinking and problem solving**, from breaking a problem into steps to writing and testing a program.
- **Programming practice** that the course assesses through a practical project.
- **Written for students**, with worked examples and exam-style questions.
- **Covers both levels**, so one book fits a class with mixed Standard and Higher Level students.

## Where it shows in Classroom Manager

The topics a course like this introduces are exactly the ones behind a program like ours: a teacher's computer talking to student computers over a network, an encrypted connection between them, and software that must behave well when something goes wrong. Reading the course book gives a student the vocabulary to follow the other books on this list.

## Who should read it

IB Computer Science students and their teachers, and anyone who wants a structured, exam-oriented tour of the subject. Check with your school which course book it uses, since publishers and editions differ.
