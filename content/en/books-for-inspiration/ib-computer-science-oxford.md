**The argument.** This is the other major course book for the revised IB Diploma Computer Science course, published by Oxford University Press for teaching that begins in 2025 and exams that begin in 2027. It is designed to support the whole course, so it works both as a classroom text and as a reference for a student studying alone.

## Ideas worth keeping

- **Matched to the subject guide**, so the chapters follow what will be taught and assessed.
- **A broad base**, from computer fundamentals and networks to problem solving and programming.
- **A reference you can keep**, useful long after the exam.
- **Authors who teach the subject**, which shows in the choice of examples.

## Where it shows in Classroom Manager

Many of the ideas on this list, such as networks, security and testing, start here. If you plan to build software for a school one day, the course book is a good first step, and the programming books below are the second.

## Who should read it

IB students and teachers choosing between course books, and parents who want to know what the course covers. Compare it with the Hodder book and ask your teacher which one the class uses.
