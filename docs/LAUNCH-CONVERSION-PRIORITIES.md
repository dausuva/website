# Highest-value next launch-page work

These priorities are a judgement about this site's buyer journey, not measured
ROI or a promise of conversion gains. Pricing, feature explanations, privacy,
deployment limits, localized pages, genuine illustrative screenshots and a clear
demo route already exist. Build on that rather than adding another generic page.

## 1. Show one real, short workflow demo

Record roughly 60–90 seconds with synthetic computers/files: prepare the lesson,
send a message or restriction, collect the work, then restore the room. Identify
the actual build and avoid claims the recording does not demonstrate. Add captions
and a short transcript; use a poster and click-to-play rather than autoplay or a
third-party embed that loads on every visit. Keep the existing screenshots for
people who do not play video. Owner input needed: the actual approved recording.

## 2. Make the pilot offer easy to approve

Provide a one-page printable pilot checklist linked from Getting started and
Request: prerequisites, one Host/one Client, school IT responsibilities, separate
Teacher/Admin keys, certificate trust, independent recovery access, representative
actions, restriction removal and agreed acceptance criteria. State what the demo
and supported deployment include and what must be agreed separately. Use the
actual supplied build's support/security limits, not a generic readiness badge.
No invented response-time or support SLA promise. Owner input: approved scope.

## 3. Publish one genuine pilot result when available

A small case study can answer “will this work in a school like ours?”: Windows
environment, approximate lab size, tested tasks, problems encountered and outcome.
Use a school's name, quote or logo only with permission. Publish time-saving
numbers only when measured with a stated baseline. Until then, keep the real
workflow evidence; never add placeholder testimonials, fabricated ratings or logos.

## 4. Learn from enquiries before adding a funnel

Check Search Console/Bing monthly and record genuine demo enquiries. Ask how
schools found the product and what nearly stopped them requesting a demo. Improve
the existing page that answers that objection. A mail-link click is not a completed
enquiry. Account creation, elaborate calculators, cookie analytics, more forms or
paid acquisition are not prerequisites for this first launch pass.

Suggested next move: record the short demo, then finalize the pilot checklist.
Review enquiry quality and owner workload before investing in more landing pages.
